Snowball Analytics

Snowball Analytics is a portfolio tracker with a strong focus on dividends and performance: dividend calendar and rating, benchmarking against indices, risk metrics such as the Sharpe ratio, and diversification that looks inside the funds you hold1. Its free plan covers 1 portfolio and 10 holdings; paid plans cost $79.99, $149.99 or $249.99 per year, with a 14-day free trial2.

AURIX

AURIX looks at the whole picture rather than one income stream. Its free plan has no asset limit and covers stocks, ETFs, funds, crypto, metals, real estate and cash. Intelligence shows which positions carry the most weight, how your wealth is split and what has changed.
