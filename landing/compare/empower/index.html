Empower

The Empower Personal Dashboard is a free financial dashboard1. You connect your accounts, from retirement plans and investments to mortgages, loans, bank accounts and credit cards, and it brings together net worth, portfolio analysis, budgeting and cash flow, and retirement planning12. It also offers access to financial professionals1.

AURIX

AURIX does not ask you to connect any account. You record your stocks, ETFs, funds, crypto, metals, real estate and cash, and Intelligence shows what your wealth depends on: which positions carry the most weight, how it is split and what has changed. It explains your own data and never tells you what to buy or sell.
