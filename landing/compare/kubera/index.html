Free plan with no asset limit. No bank or broker connection needed.

Kubera

Kubera is a very complete personal balance sheet, built for complex wealth: it connects to thousands of banks and brokerages through aggregators, tracks crypto and DeFi, real estate, alternative assets and multi-entity structures1. It costs $250/year (Essentials) or $2,500/year (Black, for multiple people, trusts and companies)2, with a 14-day trial and no permanent free plan indicated3.

AURIX

AURIX starts from a different question: what does your wealth really depend on? It brings stocks, ETFs, funds, crypto, metals, real estate and cash into one view, and Intelligence shows which positions carry the most weight, how your wealth is split and what has changed.
