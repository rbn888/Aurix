Sharesight

Sharesight is a portfolio tracker built around performance and tax reporting. It brings stocks, ETFs, dividends, precious metals, brokers and global markets into one view, and integrates with hundreds of brokers and finance apps1. Its free plan covers 1 portfolio and 10 holdings, closed ones included; paid plans run from $7 to $23.25 per month, billed annually2.

AURIX

AURIX starts from a different question: what does your wealth really depend on? Its free plan has no asset limit. It brings stocks, ETFs, funds, crypto, metals, real estate and cash into one view, and Intelligence shows which positions carry the most weight, how your wealth is split and what has changed.
